---
title: "Holder wallet"
description: "The Didit ID holder wallet — where credentials are claimed, stored, and presented with selective disclosure."
---

The holder wallet is the holder-facing half of Didit ID: it opens credential offers (from a QR code, deep link, or email claim link), runs the OpenID4VCI exchange to claim the SD-JWT VC, stores credentials bound to device keys, and answers OpenID4VP presentation requests with only the claims the holder approves.

<Note>Content lands with the wallet release.</Note>
