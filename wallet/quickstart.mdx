---
title: "Wallet quickstart"
description: "Claim your first credential into the holder wallet and present it to a verifier in a few minutes."
---

This quickstart will take you from an emailed claim link (or QR offer) to a verified presentation: open the offer in the wallet, enter the transaction code, claim the credential, then answer a verifier's presentation request while disclosing only the requested claims.

<Note>Content lands with the wallet release.</Note>
