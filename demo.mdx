This walkthrough uses the live Didit ID sandbox. You create an account in the console, issue a credential by email, claim it into the passkey-secured wallet, and present it to the VeriBank relying-party demo.

<Note>
  You need a passkey-capable browser and an email address you can read. The sandbox sends account and credential messages to that email.
</Note>

## End-to-end flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an account in the console">
    Open the [console](https://console-idv0.staging.didit.me) and create a sandbox account. New accounts trust the Didit Network framework by default, so the demo works immediately. You can later turn that framework off and require explicit trust.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Issue a credential to your email">
    Create a credential offer, choose an email recipient, and send it. The claim link goes to the holder wallet.

    {/* screenshot: console issue-by-email 1280x800 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open it in the wallet">
    Open the claim link in the [wallet](https://wallet-idv0.staging.didit.me), sign in with a passkey, review the offer, and claim the SD-JWT VC.

    {/* screenshot: wallet claim-offer 390x844 */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to the demo relying party">
    Open [VeriBank](https://veribank-idv0.staging.didit.me) and pick a scenario. The relying party creates an OpenID4VP presentation request.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Present and verify">
    Approve the requested claims in the wallet. The verifier receives only those disclosed claims and the final `verdict`.

    {/* screenshot: veribank verified-result 1280x800 */}
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you just proved

- An issuer created and emailed a standards-based credential offer.
- A holder claimed the credential into a passkey-secured wallet.
- A relying party verified the presentation with OpenID4VP, SD-JWT VC selective disclosure, lifecycle checks, and trust policy.
