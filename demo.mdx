title: "Run the full demo locally"
description: "Spin up the Didit Credentials service, console, and holder wallet on your machine and walk an issue, claim, present, and verify loop end to end."

This page will walk you through running the complete Didit Credentials stack locally — the credentials service on `http://localhost:8011`, the console, and the holder wallet — then driving one credential through its full life: create a schema, issue an offer, claim it into the wallet, present it to a verifier, and read the verification result.

<Note>Content lands with the wallet release.</Note>
