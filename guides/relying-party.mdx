---
title: "Register a relying party"
description: "Register verifier origins with the relying-party registry so presentation requests over the Digital Credentials API are origin-checked."
---

This guide will cover the relying-party registry: registering a verifier's web origin and `client_id` with `POST /v1/relying-parties`, how the registered origin is checked when the verifier requests a credential presentation over the W3C Digital Credentials API, and how to list and revoke registrations.

<Note>Content lands with the wallet release.</Note>
